Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This website

getportside.com sets no first-party cookies and runs no analytics or ad tracking.

To power checkout on the landing page, this site loads Paddle’s script (Paddle.js) from cdn.paddle.com. When you buy Portside, checkout is handled by Paddle, our merchant of record. Paddle processes your payment details under Paddle’s privacy policy; we never see your card number. We receive your email address and transaction ID so we can create your Portside account and help you later.

The app

Portside has no telemetry and no analytics. It never sends your files, file names, folders, or usage anywhere.

During the trial, Portside does not contact the license server unless you ask to sign in or reset a password. Once licensed, Portside communicates with license.getportside.com (hosted on Cloudflare Workers and D1) to let you sign in, reset your password, and run a weekly license check that keeps one license active on one Mac at a time. That service stores your email address, a salted verifier derived from your client-side password prehash (never a plaintext password), your Paddle order references, scrambled hashes of your Mac’s hardware UUID (never your serial number), license timestamps, and short-lived IP and account timestamps used only for rate limiting.

Account setup and password-reset emails are sent from license@getportside.com through Resend. After you grant permission (or click Check for Updates…), update checks read the feed on getportside.com and download releases you approve from GitHub. Anything you run in the built-in terminal or transfer over SFTP or SMB is your own traffic, initiated by you.

Email

If you write to support@getportside.com, we use your address to reply and for nothing else.
